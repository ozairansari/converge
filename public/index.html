THE RULES, CLEARLY

One simple idea.
Eight iterations.

  1. Everyone secretly chooses an unused whole number from 0 to 100 within 20 seconds.
  2. The server averages submitted choices, then multiplies by the factor shown for the round. Targets are capped at 100.
  3. Score max(0, 1000 − 20 × distance), rounded to a whole number, plus 250 if closest. Equal closest guesses share the bonus.
  4. Factors cycle through ⅔, 4/3, 0.8, 1.2. Each number can be used once per match.
  5. Missed choices score zero. Fewer than two submissions means no points. After 8 rounds, the top total wins. Ties share the win.
